## Going Back

The last week of the season the light changes before the weather does. It comes in
lower and later and lies along the grass instead of falling on it, and the whole
marsh takes on the look of a thing being packed away.

I have four notebooks, most of them wet at some point and dried badly. Reading
them back I find I recorded the same five things over and over and thought each
time that I had noticed something new.

Perhaps that is what a season is. Not five hundred observations but five, looked
at until they open. The heron is still fishing the second pool. The water is still
refusing the stakes.
