## Grey Weather

Three days of low cloud and the marsh loses its colours one at a time. First the
greens go, then the browns, and by the third afternoon the whole basin is a single
soft grey in which only movement is visible.

This is not a loss. In colour the eye is lazy and takes the whole scene at once;
in grey it must work, and working, it finds things it had walked past all week —
the seam where the old channel ran, the slight doming of ground where something
was built and taken away again long before anyone thought to write it down.

The wind comes off the water without hurry. Everything here is patient. I am
learning it slowly and against my nature.
