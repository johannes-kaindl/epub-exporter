## The Heron

There is a heron who fishes the second pool and has decided I am furniture. He
lands while I am writing, folds himself down to half his height, and waits with a
concentration I have never managed at any desk.

He is not still. That is the thing one gets wrong from photographs. He is
continuously adjusting, a fraction at a time, the way a held note is continuously
adjusted to stay in tune. Stillness in a living thing is work.

When he strikes I always miss it. I have watched him take perhaps forty fish this
season and I have not once seen the moment itself, only the before and the after,
and the water closing over the difference.
